# Source attribution and reuse terms

ECtHR-NPD is derived from publicly available judgments of the European Court of
Human Rights in HUDOC. This package contains derived tabular records and does
not redistribute raw judgment text. HUDOC materials remain subject to the
Court's copyright, disclaimer and database terms.

This distribution does not add an open data or code licence, grant rights in source
judgments, or certify institutional/legal clearance. No licence should be inferred
from the fact that a repository can be downloaded. Consult the rights holder
about permission for the intended reuse and cite the paper and exact revision.

Applicant names are masked. Original HUDOC case identifiers, full judgment dates,
exact awards and demographics ARE retained at the author's request. This package
is directly linkable to public judgments and is NOT an anonymous dataset.
Private crosswalks, raw judgments, extraction logs and internal review materials
are not included. Publication was authorized by the authors; it is not a statement of legal or institutional approval.
