# Dataset Card

ECtHR-NPD is a legal NLP benchmark for formula-free legal regression:
predicting case-level non-pecuniary damage awards at the European Court
of Human Rights.

The public release contains a canonical case-level table with validated
targets, chronological split/view tags, leakage-audited structured
annotations, external macroeconomic covariates, and public HUDOC
identifiers. Official judgments are not redistributed.

This dataset is intended for research benchmarking and empirical legal
analysis. It should not be used for legal advice, settlement anchoring,
or automated judicial decision-making.
