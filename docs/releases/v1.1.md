# Five case-level eligibility changes in v1.1

This is a release explanation, not an internal audit ledger. Only project-assigned
case IDs are listed. Names, official case numbers, document quotations, source
anchors and the identifier crosswalk are not distributed.

v1.0 retains the paper cohort (14,575 cases). v1.1 has 14,570 cases: three records
are excluded because the available award is not separable into a pure NPD amount;
two are quarantined because a complete case-level NPD target cannot currently be
supported. Quarantine does not mean a zero award or permanent ineligibility.

| Project case ID | Issue established from retained source review | v1.1 treatment | What would support reconsideration? |
|---|---|---|---|
| case_04358 | Per-beneficiary sums combine non-pecuniary damage and costs/expenses without a separate NPD component. Summing beneficiaries does not separate the heads. | Excluded: mixed heads. | Source evidence specifying a separable pure NPD total. |
| case_10232 | Per-person awards combine NPD and costs/expenses and are subject to deductions for domestic payments; they do not supply an unambiguous pure case-level NPD target. | Excluded: mixed heads. | Separate NPD components and a supported treatment of deductions. |
| case_01477 | A global award covers NPD and costs/expenses without disaggregation. The global sum cannot be labelled pure NPD. | Excluded: mixed heads. | A source-supported NPD-only component. |
| case_01529 | The reasoning and operative wording do not establish a consistent compensation-head interpretation; beneficiary count and per-person versus case-total scope are unresolved. | Quarantined: head and unit-of-award conflict. | Consistent source evidence for both the head and complete case-level total. |
| case_12956 | The retained zero label is contradicted by an established positive NPD component, but that component alone does not prove the complete case total. | Quarantined: incomplete target. | Verification of all relevant award components and the complete NPD total. |

## Consequences for counts and interpretation

- Three train cases, one validation case and one test case are removed from v1.1.
- The removed test case belongs to Challenging: 699 becomes 698. No surviving
  case changes split or Challenging membership; the selector rule is unchanged.
- The associated 24 non-placeholder applicant source records are outside the
  v1.1 cohort: 44,581 becomes 44,557. Do not treat these as 24 confirmed unique people.
- v1.0 retains these records with `mixed_head_reference_label` or
  `unresolved_reference_label` in `target_status`. Their applicant amounts are
  withheld under `reference_case_target_unresolved`, not filled with zero.
- These five removals are separate from the two authorised amount corrections
  already applied to both v1.0 and v1.1. Do not interpret the total case-count
  difference as seven removed cases.
- v1.0 preserves the paper cohort but includes the two later corrections; it is
  not a byte-identical original experiment input. v1.1 numbers must not be
  substituted for the paper's reported cohort or model results.

## What this does not claim

This targeted review does not certify that every remaining extraction is free
from error. Missing individual allocations, joint awards and incomplete personal
links are not automatically case-total errors, and no equal-share imputation was
used to force applicant sums to equal case awards. Feedback using project case
IDs is welcome; keep identifying source evidence out of public issue reports.
