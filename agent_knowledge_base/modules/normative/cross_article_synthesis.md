# Cross-Article Synthesis

Multi-article cases should not be handled by naive addition.

## Main Patterns

### `single_article`

- only one article meaningfully drives the NPD outcome

### `additive`

- distinct harms support separate contribution logic
- example: liberty harm plus fair-trial harm

### `absorbed`

- a secondary article is accessory and largely covered by the primary one
- common with Articles 13 and 14

### `overlap_adjusted`

- multiple serious articles are present but the suffering overlaps
- avoid double counting

### `global_single_award`

- the Court would plausibly treat the totality of violations as one combined NPD
  assessment

## Accessory Articles

### Article 13

- often parasitic on the primary substantive or procedural article
- usually contributes through synthesis, not through an independent amount path

### Article 14

- often modifies the gravity or character of the primary article
- do not assume a fully separate amount path

## Output Requirement

The synthesizer must output a named cross-article pattern and explain why that
pattern was selected.
