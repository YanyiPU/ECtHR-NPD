# Article 13 Module: Effective Remedy

## Role

Article 13 is usually accessory.

It often modifies or reinforces the primary article rather than generating a
fully independent NPD amount path.

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is possible, but do not assume Article 13 produces a separate
positive amount on its own.

Zero is more plausible when:

- the primary article already dominates the harm assessment
- the remedy failure adds limited distinct non-pecuniary suffering

## Assessment Focus

- identify the parent article
- assess whether the lack of remedy adds distinct suffering
- prefer synthesis logic over isolated amount logic
