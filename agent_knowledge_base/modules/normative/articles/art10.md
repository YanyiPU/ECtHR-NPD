# Article 10 Module: Freedom of Expression

## Common Subtypes

- political speech restriction
- journalistic reporting restriction
- criminal prosecution for expression
- fines or sanctions
- censorship or prior restraint
- dismissal or retaliation for expression

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is more plausible when:

- the interference is serious or punitive
- imprisonment or criminal exposure exists
- public-interest or journalistic speech is central

Zero is more plausible when:

- the violation is narrow and relatively low-impact
- harm is weakly individualized

## Assessment Focus

- context of expression
- severity of interference
- chilling effect
- overlap with Article 11 or 6
