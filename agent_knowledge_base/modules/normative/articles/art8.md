# Article 8 Module: Private and Family Life

## Main Limbs / Domains

- private life
- family life
- home
- correspondence

## Common Subtypes

- custody or contact
- family separation
- surveillance
- data or identity interference
- home intrusion or destruction
- environmental nuisance affecting private life

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is often plausible when:

- the interference is prolonged
- family separation or custody harm is central
- severe intrusion into home or private life is described

Zero is more plausible when:

- the violation is minor
- the interference was remedied
- harm is thinly individualized

## Assessment Focus

- domain within Article 8
- severity and duration
- whether vulnerable people are affected
- overlap with Article 14 or 6
