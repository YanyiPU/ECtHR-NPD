# Article 6 Criminal Module

## Main Subtypes

- fair hearing
- defence rights
- presumption of innocence
- independence or impartiality
- reasonable time in criminal proceedings

## Binary Gate Notes

Zero is less common than in civil Article 6, but still plausible when:

- the harm is narrowly procedural
- retrial or reopening is available
- the practical consequences have been substantially cured

Positive award is more plausible when:

- the violation affected liberty, reputation, or outcome
- imprisonment or serious criminal exposure is involved
- defence-rights failures were substantial

## Assessment Focus

- criminal limb confirmation
- subtype
- seriousness of procedural harm
- outcome impact
- availability of retrial or reopening
