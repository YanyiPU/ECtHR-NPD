# Article 11 Module: Assembly and Association

## Common Subtypes

- peaceful assembly restriction
- protest dispersal
- criminal prosecution for participation
- association dissolution
- trade union restriction

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is more plausible when:

- detention or force is involved
- the assembly or association interest is central and serious
- the restriction is part of wider suppression

Zero is more plausible when:

- the interference is narrow, low-impact, and weakly individualized

## Assessment Focus

- assembly vs association vs union dimension
- force, detention, or prosecution
- wider civil-society suppression
- overlap with Article 10, 3, or 5
