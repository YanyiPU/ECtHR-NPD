# Article 5 Module: Liberty and Security

## Main Limbs

- lawfulness of detention
- procedural review and challenge rights
- compensation-right violations under Article 5

## Common Subtypes

- unlawful detention
- no reasons for arrest
- excessive pre-trial detention
- no effective review
- no compensation right

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is often plausible when:

- liberty deprivation is central
- detention lasted long enough to generate individualized distress

Zero becomes more plausible when:

- the violation is narrow and procedural
- domestic redress already exists
- the harm looks minor relative to classic detention cases

## Assessment Focus

- detention length
- whether liberty loss was real or merely procedural
- domestic redress
- overlap with Article 3 or 6
