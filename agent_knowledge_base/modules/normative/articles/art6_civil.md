# Article 6 Civil Module

## Main Subtypes

- reasonable time
- access to court
- fair hearing
- independence or impartiality
- enforcement-related fairness

## Binary Gate Notes

Article 6 civil is one of the most important zero-risk settings.

Zero is more plausible when:

- the violation is mainly procedural
- delay is relatively modest
- retrial, reopening, or equivalent redress exists
- harm is not strongly individualized

Positive award is more plausible when:

- delay is very long
- the applicant suffered clear individualized non-pecuniary harm
- the procedural defect had serious practical consequences

## Assessment Focus

- civil limb confirmation
- subtype
- duration of proceedings if relevant
- remedy already available
- why the case should or should not exit via the binary zero path
