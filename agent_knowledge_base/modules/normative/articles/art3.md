# Article 3 Module: Ill-Treatment

## Main Limbs

- `substantive`
- `procedural`

## Common Subtypes

- torture
- inhuman treatment
- degrading treatment
- detention conditions
- medical neglect in detention
- failure to investigate ill-treatment

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is usually strong when:

- substantive Article 3 harm is present
- physical or psychological suffering is individualized
- detention conditions or abuse are serious

Zero is less plausible when:

- torture or serious inhuman treatment is central
- lasting consequences are described

## Assessment Focus

- qualification severity
- direct state-agent involvement vs positive obligation failure
- lasting consequences
- vulnerability
- repeated episodes or concealment
- overlap with Article 5
