# Article 2 Module: Right to Life

## Main Limbs

- `substantive`
- `procedural`

## Common Subtypes

- deliberate killing by state agents
- disappearance
- failure to protect from known risk
- healthcare or regulatory failure
- life-endangerment without death
- investigative failure

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is usually strong when:

- death or near-death harm is central
- close family suffering is individualized
- substantive harm is present

Zero is less plausible when:

- there is severe death-related harm
- the case concerns bereavement, disappearance, or grave risk to life

## Assessment Focus

- limb
- subtype
- relationship of applicant to victim
- gravity of harm
- concealment or prolonged impunity
- overlap with Article 13 or 14
