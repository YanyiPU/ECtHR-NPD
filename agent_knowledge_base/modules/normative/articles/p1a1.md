# P1-1 Module: Protection of Property

## Common Subtypes

- deprivation of property
- control of use
- peaceful enjoyment interference
- pensions or social-security entitlements
- licensing or business-interest interference
- enforcement-related possession harm

## Binary Gate Notes

This article often has stronger pecuniary than non-pecuniary dimensions.

Zero is more plausible when:

- the case is mainly pecuniary in character
- the facts do not show strong individualized distress beyond economic loss

Positive NPD is more plausible when:

- the interference caused substantial personal disruption, prolonged insecurity,
  or non-economic suffering

## Assessment Focus

- distinguish economic loss from non-pecuniary suffering
- avoid treating property interference as automatic NPD
- assess overlap with Article 14 or enforcement-related Article 6 issues
