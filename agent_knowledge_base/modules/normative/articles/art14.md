# Article 14 Module: Discrimination

## Role

Article 14 is generally accessory to another Convention right.

Its main function in this KB is to modify the gravity and character of the
primary article rather than to create a fully independent amount path by
default.

## Binary Gate Notes

Positive award is more plausible when:

- discrimination is central, explicit, and individualized
- the discriminatory character intensifies the primary harm

Zero is more plausible when:

- the discrimination finding is formally present but adds little distinct
  suffering beyond the primary article

## Assessment Focus

- identify the linked article
- identify the protected characteristic if possible
- assess whether discrimination is central or ancillary
- use synthesis to avoid double counting
