# Finding-Sufficient Guidance

This module is about cases where the Court may treat the finding of a violation
as adequate just satisfaction for NPD.

## High-Risk Settings

### Article 6

Particularly common when:

- delay is relatively modest
- the harm is mainly procedural
- retrial or reopening is available or already ordered
- the violation is real but the monetary harm is not strongly individualized

### Other Procedural Violations

More plausible when:

- the violation is technical or limited
- there is little concrete non-pecuniary harm described
- another remedy already addresses the practical harm

## Low-Risk Settings

Finding sufficient is much less plausible when:

- Article 2 substantive harm is involved
- Article 3 substantive harm is involved
- detention, physical suffering, or severe mental suffering is central
- the facts show strong individualized distress and no equivalent redress

## Regression Use

This module should guide continuous amount calibration.

It should not be used as a shortcut to force zero in serious substantive cases.
